05 · Strategic recommendations

Actions grounded in respondent signals

Recommendations are direct implications of the uploaded survey findings.

01

Lead with cross-format creation

Center the product story on one workflow for both image and video creation, supported by powerful editing capability.

49.2% cross-format motivation · 46.3% powerful features
02

Prioritize practical AI utility

Make auto adjust, text-to-speech, and smart search prominent in discovery and onboarding experiences.

43.5% · 43.2% · 36.8% respectively
03

Reduce switching friction

Design adoption journeys around users already comfortable with other editing software.

44.4% cite existing software habits
04

Broaden the CapCut frame

Demonstrate photo, professional, desktop, and non-TikTok use cases to address recurring product perceptions.

20.0% TikTok-only · 16.7% no photo editing · 15.6% not professional
05

Meet creators where they publish

Use workflows and examples oriented to Facebook and Instagram, the two dominant posting platforms in the sample.

79.1% Facebook · 58.6% Instagram
06

Build business-offering visibility

Increase recognition of CapCut for Business while connecting business use cases to the broader product promise.
